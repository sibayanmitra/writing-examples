## Why do you want to work on this project? (Max 100 words)

I am currently working on the topic of eval awareness and its effects in languages other than English.
In one of our experiments, we tried to steer the model to evaluation direction while in deployment to see if that increases refusal in languages other than English. It increased refusal in few models and did not in few, although the direction was detectable with high auroc. This is mostly due to the fact the models are trained differently and act differently during deployment and evaluation.
